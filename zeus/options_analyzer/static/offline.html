⚡ 连不上电脑上的 ZEUS

Can't reach ZEUS on your computer
  • 电脑睡眠或关机了：唤醒它，并在 ⚙ 设置 → 手机访问 里打开「电脑不睡眠」。
    The computer is asleep or off: wake it, and turn on "Keep this computer awake" in ⚙ Settings → Phone access.
  • ZEUS 没在运行：在电脑上重新启动 ZEUS。
    ZEUS is not running: start it again on the computer.
  • 手机或电脑上的 Tailscale 没连上：打开 Tailscale App，确认已连接。
    Tailscale is off on the phone or the computer: open the Tailscale app and connect.
